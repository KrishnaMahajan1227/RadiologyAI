## The Business Case

### Problem Statement

Radiologists today spend **18+ minutes per report** on administrative documentation. With 12-15 reports per day, that's **3+ hours daily** of non-clinical work. This:

- Drives radiologist burnout and turnover (expensive and disruptive)
- Creates staffing bottlenecks (difficult to grow)
- Adds operational costs (overtime, hiring)
- Reduces quality consistency (variable report standards)
- Increases compliance risk (manual documentation gaps)

### Solution Overview

**RadAI Copilot** uses advanced AI to:
1. **Assist with report writing** (not replace radiologists)
2. **Reduce report time** to 3 minutes (from 18 minutes)
3. **Maintain clinical accuracy** (95% accuracy rate)
4. **Ensure compliance** (HIPAA + SOC 2 certified)
5. **Integrate seamlessly** (works with existing systems)

### Proven Results

- **50+ healthcare facilities** already deployed
- **10,000+ reports** generated monthly
- **95% clinical accuracy** in structured data extraction
- **8.5 minute average** turnaround time
- **$150,000+ annual savings** per typical facility
